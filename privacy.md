# Privacy Policy: AIRCON G.O.D

**Last updated: October 16, 2026**

This policy explains what data the AIRCON G.O.D Discord bot ("the Bot") collects and how it is used.

## 1. Data We Collect
- Discord server (guild) IDs and channel IDs
- Discord user IDs of people who use Bot commands
- Reminder content and schedules that admins create
- Basic command usage needed for the Bot to work

We do **not** collect passwords, payment information, or private messages.

## 2. How We Use Data
Data is used only to run the Bot's features: sending reminders, managing schedules, and responding to commands.

## 3. Data Sharing
We do not sell or share your data with third parties. Data may be disclosed only if required by law.

## 4. Data Storage and Retention
Data is stored securely and kept only as long as needed to operate the Bot. When the Bot is removed from a server, that server's data may be deleted.

## 5. Data Deletion
To request deletion of your data or your server's data, contact us at [your email or Discord username]. We will process requests within a reasonable time.

## 6. Children's Privacy
The Bot is not intended for users under the minimum age required by Discord's Terms of Service.

## 7. Changes
We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version.
